POP Help

POP estimates wind and wand parameters from bubble photos or lets you explore forward simulations. This page summarizes how to operate the tools and the meaning of each parameter.

Operation

  • Analyzer: Upload an image, drag to fit an ellipse, optionally set scale (📏) then press Analyze to estimate wind.
  • Simulator: Adjust wind speed, direction, jerk and bubble size to see the predicted ellipse.
  • Model parameters: Use the sliders to tune correlations and physical constants.

Parameter reference

Defaults shown in parentheses.

  • c₁ (0.90) – slope of axis-ratio vs Weber correlation.
  • c₂ (0.35) – saturation constant of axis-ratio correlation.
  • ρ (1.20 kg/m³) – air density.
  • σ (0.030 N/m) – bubble surface tension.
  • μair (1.8×10⁻⁵ Pa·s) – dynamic viscosity of air.
  • μfilm (1.0×10⁻³ Pa·s) – viscosity of the bubble film.
  • R₀ (0.20 m) – fallback bubble radius when image scale is unknown.

Key relations: We = ρ U² R / σ, χ(We) = 1 + c₁·We/(1 + c₂·We), and relaxation time τ ≈ μair·R/σ.
